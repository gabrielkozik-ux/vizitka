Lying to Your Lawyer? Why Even the Best Attorney Can't Save You in Court

Published: June 4, 2026 | Author: Mgr. Gabriel Kožík | Podcast IUSTORIA

Your attorney is bound by confidentiality and always on your side. Yet the same scenario repeats in practice: the client withholds inconvenient facts — and they surface in court at the worst possible moment.

In this episode of the IUSTORIA law firm podcast (in Czech), we talk about why complete honesty with your attorney is the foundation of any litigation strategy, what attorney-client confidentiality actually covers, and what a dispute looks like when the other side knows more about your case than your own lawyer.
