What data and why

When you write to me through the form or by email, I process your name, email address, the content of your message and any information you share in it. I use it only to reply to you and, where relevant, to arrange further cooperation.

Legal basis

If you are interested in my services, the processing is a step taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For other enquiries, it is my legitimate interest in being able to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Confidentiality

I treat the information you entrust to me as confidential and handle it in line with an attorney's duty of confidentiality under Section 21 of Act No. 85/1996 Coll., on the Legal Profession.

How long

If we do not start working together, I will delete your data no later than 12 months after our last communication. If we do, I keep it for the period required by law and by the rules of the legal profession.

Who else has access

Messages from the form are stored in Google Workspace, operated by Google Ireland Limited as my processor. Data may also be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the transfer is further covered by standard contractual clauses. I do not share or sell your data to anyone else.

Analytics cookies

If you consent, I use Google Analytics 4 on this website. It tells me how many people visit, which pages they read and whether they get in touch (form, booking, phone, email). It processes information about your browser and device, your approximate location at country or region level and a pseudonymous identifier stored in cookies. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll., on Electronic Communications). The data is stored by Google Ireland Limited as my processor for 14 months. It may be transferred to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can withdraw your consent at any time via the Cookie settings link in the footer. Without your consent, Google Analytics does not run.

YouTube videos

Some articles include an embedded YouTube video. The player only loads after you click on the video; until then, nothing is sent to Google. After you click, your browser connects to the servers of Google Ireland Limited, which may process your IP address and device data and store identifiers on your device. I use the youtube-nocookie.com mode. By clicking on the video, you consent to it being loaded (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll.). Further processing is Google's responsibility under its privacy policy.

Website hosting

This website runs on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.). On every visit, your IP address and technical data about the request are processed so that the website can be delivered and protected against attacks. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in operating the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Booking a meeting

If you book a meeting via Google Calendar, you enter your name and email address in a booking form operated by Google Ireland Limited as my processor within Google Workspace. I use this data only to arrange and hold the meeting (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its processing and to data portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Just write to me at info@gabrielkozik.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.gov.cz).

Providing your data is voluntary, but without contact details I cannot reply to you. I do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.
